Paintings III: 'S', "Ukrainian Alphabet" by Narbut

August 28, 2026

Why are you smirking?
Standing in a snowcapped plateau
Side-eyeing the scribbler scratching your portrait in pencil
While you tower over the surrounding pine trees
You cannot hide
Your leathery hide of wrinkles and gray
Your pose like a candid photograph
In this cold, the scribbler must have shivered
Did you comfort them?
Did you mean to stumble upon them,
Or did their makeshift campsite surprise you
Like it surely surprised them?
They weren't rushing.
They probably left the makeshift table
To relieve themself in a makeshift toilet
When suddenly they turned and saw you smirking.
The tingling felt in my toes isn't frostbite.
This feeling of discovery is all at once exciting and terrifying.
I did not come for you,
But seeing you
Squashes my sense of purpose.
Why else did I come
If not for you?
I take my simple pencil
My humble sketchpad
And get to the act
Of catching
That embarrassed smirk
In stark lines
And once I start sketching,
I forget totally why I'd come
At all.
I raise my gaze to savor your form
But in a blink
You're gone
And the falling snow
Coats my makeshift table like a blanket
And fills the empty spaces
Where you once stood
Smirking.

Paintings II: "Marynka Smoking" by Kitaj

August 26, 2026

Is she smoking, or is she smoking?
Her shadow pools where she sits on bed,
Yellow mattress stained with spots of red.
What's beyond the open door?
A bathroom, perhaps, blackened by the unlit light.
No fear of monsters,
No monsters but her, perhaps.
A tempest of tousled hair tied up in a bun,
Primed for
Smoking.
Mustard bed with ketchup stains,
The fit woman with folds of flesh in shadow
She breathes a plume of sky-blue smoke, the breath of relief
She's looking at me coldly
Over-the-shoulderly
Wholly aware that what we've done
In this bed we've made
Will never be spoken
What matters anyway?
All she knows is heartbreak
Because she breaks hearts
When she looks away, she maybe whispers that she'll draw a bath
I sit in her chair, on her bed, among her clothes
Heaped on the rough carpet floor
Where I indulge in her scent, a bouquet of sweat and smoke and sex
I sip her wine, I drink it by the gallon
I hum a song with words I'll never remember
I hear her sing along
Isn't it good?
Is she smoking, or is she smoking?
Either way, I'm burning alive.

Paintings I: "Bathsheba at Her Bath" by Veronese

August 25, 2026

Wave or cloud unclear, the top-right shore-sky
Roiling.
They call her bathing intimate; I see her guarded.
Wrapped in surf, clutching chest, looking
Up, at, over and beyond
The red man.

One hand gestures
Eyebrows deeply furrowed
Head tilted down, staring at the unassuming woman.

Alarming is what it is.

Trapped beneath the gaze of two stony men,
One cracking.
One might consider diving, full-clothed, headfirst
Plunging to the depths of the bath
Taking on and in the defiled waters
Transforming into a tiny loathed slug and
Riding to the ocean the currents of the drain.

So he's standing, right,
The red man,
One gesturing hand suggesting the plunge
Smiling as if to say, "Only remove those robes first."

Lust is in the eyes staring down upon the woman,
The poor creature.
"It's none of your business, my sitting," her right hand signals.
She's looking through him,
The red man,
To the shore-sky tide recoiling.

Trickle Down

July 28, 2026

Sunday call, squatting in the squalor,
Titanium wrench tight-clenched iron grip.
Twist the plastic clamp, close the collar,
Hear the steady flow of dollars drip.

Flip the main breaker switch, kill the power,
Cut the drywall, isolate and snip
The problematic wire; should take one hour.
Hear the steady flow of dollars drip.

Dig the corner holes and plant the posts
In Portland Cement. To align, slip
A level line end-to-end, then mount pickets.
Hear the steady flow of dollars drip.

Broken wrench, replace it in an instant.
Copper fire, jealousy won't fix it.
Falling fence, the cost of doing business.
Hear the steady flow of dollars drip.

Fire

July 23, 2026

Tender are the flames of aging fire,
Who, with steady breathing, sparks new fire.

Kindling, coal, and gasoline ignite
And burn differently, and yet the fire

That consumes them produces light and
Heat, leaving only a hint of fire.

Maintenance. Caring is maintenance:
Take caution when you maintain the fire.

Feed it when it needs more nourishment;
Be careful not to indulge the fire

Because it will indulge. Remember,
Jimmy, what it costs to play with fire.

Commune: A Glosa

July 17, 2026

"Shout unto God with the voice of triumph.
For the Lord most high is terrible;
He is a great King over all the earth.
He shall subdue the people under us."
-Psalm 47

---

Screaming overhead, the Behemoth's blue
Belly a blemish on the firmament's
Countenance concealed by dust, armament
Free-falling and caterwauling down to
Ground level where settled dust erupts. Who
Piloted the Beast, Man or Machine? Month
After month bombardment ain't enough.
Seventy-five thousand dead, among them
Doctors, lawyers, men, women, and children.
Shout unto God with the voice of triumph.

In pleasant company, we give our thanks,
Grateful that the open sky is sunny
And bright. A cousin, troubled with money,
Opines the genocide of Gaza stinks
Of Jewish money, or rather he thinks
The Jews are paying to make parables
Of Gazan plight. In paradoxical
Fashion, he imagines the oppressor,
Israel, is Yahweh's true successor.
True. For the Lord most high is terrible.

His rebuttal? You should read more Scripture.
Nevermind the lessons one learns from songs,
Open up the Bible and read from Psalms.
Listen to them longing for the rapture,
Not the snakes that slither in the pasture,
Cursed as they are to wallow in their dearth
Swallowing dirt and longing for a hearth
To lie beside and warm their cold, cold blood.
Don't let the Lord, God, be misunderstood:
He is a great King over all the earth.

To read or not to read, that's my question.
There's more to words that what is on a page.
God is but an artifact of an age
When questions meant excommunication,
"God is dead" meant treason or sedition,
Writers had to qualify their bias
Writing simile and song to guide us
And criticize what critics never could.
Belief in God is not belief in good:
He shall subdue the people under us.

Missing Mr. Mayfree

July 13, 2026

Mr. Mayfree waved, he raised his right hand,
Elbow two protruding bones protracted 90 degrees
Straining to straighten, swinging narrowly 80 to 100,
An inverted pendulum of burnished bronze, melting in the
High heat.

An odd old fella.
A cold and spotted mortadella man,
Colostomy bag on rolling stand
Wheeled through rolling sand
The ground beneath his feet a blur of static
Against the coarse grains
Until wheelchair frame conquers concrete
Driveway and,
In his home safe,
The medical aid drives away.

Laura wheels him to the porch railing
Where he watches
Every morning
His shadow shrink.
He told me once the morning helped him think;
He told the time, his shadow was his marker.
The rumble in the future wasn't thunder.

I almost missed him later
'Til I caught his shadow
Waving with the trees.

Before Long

July 9, 2026

Before long, I'll have a song for every season,
But even though my evenings are my own,
Every nimbus rains without a reason
Like every chorus cricket cries alone.
Death befits the punishment for treason —
Behold! The field mouse, turned against the Crown,
Seizes means of eating, poison eats him;
Behold! The guillotine with blade unhoned
And lever-loaded spring that holds the cheese in;
Behold! The Victor box where mice atone
At peanut butter shrines that shock to seizing —

Edgelord

June 21, 2026

The monster terrorizing me the most
Is neither old folklorean cryptid
Nor holographic visage like a ghost;

Neither is it found in homes we visit,
Where gracious hosts repurpose platitudes,
Plating them on platters, lifting spirits,

Giving thanks, and expecting gratitude;
Neither is it conjured elemental;
Neither is it simply the altitude;

Neither is it feeling sentimental
Longing -- that isolated loneliness
Wherein companions act charitable

'Til charity is needed (Holiness
Disguised), then shut their hearts, minds, and wallets;
No, each of these are but the lowliest

Of crimes. Tolerable, if I'm honest.
There's only one that pains my lungs to breathe,
Only one I deign to call a prophet.

There's only one that this could ever be:
I am the monster terrorizing me.

He Who Conquers, Conquered

June 20, 2026

Penitent patient, pillow a potent power,
Prayer to the the prayer's pay-per-the-hour power.

Purple protector, supple supplier of sleep,
Keep me breathing, releasing this awful power.

Crush me like velvet. Villify this villainess
Who, under duress, undressed and blessed my power.

I confess. I relapse to the Goddess of the Gaps.
Athena may rest, Arachne knew best: Power.

There's no sacred sigil, there's no secret potion,
There is only violence, dominance, power.

Granted by the minute like a genie, bottled
For eternity. Bounded eternal power.

The notion that Pandora's box could be opened
Yet misunderstood is an alluring power.

"I can fix her," one might say. "Mending broken locks
Is all I know. I'm the key. I have the power."

But I am no key. Jimmy the Meek. Pariah.
Pious Dutton. Lover. Rejector of Power.

Ritual ala Rondeau

June 18, 2026

Hegemonic or demonic, pray
The ritual, the pipe smoking gray
And lavenderly clouds of incense,
Calms your anxious nerves and your intense
But manageable pain. Those headaches

From crowded thoughts you can't shoo away
Brighten up the sun but darken day.
Crawling out of bed feels too immense.
Hegemonic or demonic, pray.

Breathe it deeply. Hold it, let it stay,
Then exhale and watch it dissipate.
Gentle wings alleviate and lift
From fog to cloud, offering a glimpse
Of peacefulness. But you won't escape
The ritual, the pipe smoking gray.
