Prepare the Bed

July 14, 2026

Lying brain
Dead man's
Breath swells,
Then fermata.

Held in silence,
Compelled to contradict nature,
He bellows
Fiery clouds of sulfur yellow
In jet streams.

The sweat sticks
Black plastic arm
Gray folding rocking chair
Facing yard
Patio crowded
Raided widow web clusters abandoned
Empty Pots, meet Leggy Tomatoes
	Tripping and falling and thirsty
	And eager to fruit
	And prematurely stunted
The clay is soft
	Sticks and clogs tiller

Must prepare the bed
Before the garden

Missing Mr. Mayfree

July 13, 2026

Mr. Mayfree waved, he raised his right hand,
Elbow two protruding bones protracted 90 degrees
Straining to straighten, swinging narrowly 80 to 100,
An inverted pendulum of burnished bronze, melting in the
High heat.

An odd old fella.
A cold and spotted mortadella man,
Colostomy bag on rolling stand
Wheeled through rolling sand
The ground beneath his feet a blur of static
Against the coarse grains
Until wheelchair frame conquers concrete
Driveway and,
In his home safe,
The medical aid drives away.

Laura wheels him to the porch railing
Where he watches
Every morning
His shadow shrink.
He told me once the morning helped him think;
He told the time, his shadow was his marker.
The rumble in the future wasn't thunder.

I almost missed him later
'Til I caught his shadow
Waving with the trees.

Before Long

July 9, 2026

Before long, I'll have a song for every season,
But even though my evenings are my own,
Every nimbus rains without a reason
Like every chorus cricket cries alone.
Death befits the punishment for treason —
Behold! The field mouse, turned against the Crown,
Seizes means of eating, poison eats him;
Behold! The guillotine with blade unhoned
And lever-loaded spring that holds the cheese in;
Behold! The Victor box where mice atone
At peanut butter shrines that shock to seizing —
Whether by God's hands, or by one's own.

Carpet

July 7, 2026

Loose sandpaper, the forest of fibers
Scratching my feet, both cracked and dry
And flaming, desperate for a soothing balm
Like a desert parched for rain;

Like trees felled, the carpet collapses
In silence.
Perhaps a great hunter
	Like Eurydice the Cat,
	The Queen grown fat
	In her advanced age
		Of venomous fangs
		Of honed needles and grip strength
	A graceless beast
	Whose lazy days confound fools when,
		In blazing fury,
	She bounds for freedom
	Through narrowly cracked doors
	To pounce head-first in the grass
		To frolic, flop, and feast
	Before she rebounds inside
		To lounge around her dinner bowl
		To dine —
Perhaps she would hear
Them fall.

I settle into my seat
Feet planted firmly in the dirt and debris
Of the microbe colony
My step
Destroyed.

Tiny dinos in the tall, tall grass
Hiding from the hairless apes

Feeds On

July 1, 2026

Come wading through the weeds as tall as me,
Where rabbits, snakes, and crawfish congregate,
Where mud meets boot to kiss and conjugate
And suck it off your foot before you leave;
Beware the hidden danger of the leaves
Upon the melon vines that climb the grate:
Obscure them from the hens before they're ate,
Or mourn the cucumbers you'll never see.

Go rest beneath the dandelion's shade;
Go climb the mighty mountain made by ants;
Go plumb the secret tunnels underground,
And don't return until your fortune's made;
Remember, you were fortunate to dance
With fury, curiosity, and sound.

Edgelord

June 21, 2026

The monster terrorizing me the most
Is neither old folklorean cryptid
Nor holographic visage like a ghost;

Neither is it found in homes we visit,
Where gracious hosts repurpose platitudes,
Plating them on platters, lifting spirits,

Giving thanks, and expecting gratitude;
Neither is it conjured elemental;
Neither is it simply the altitude;

Neither is it feeling sentimental
Longing -- that isolated loneliness
Wherein companions act charitable

'Til charity is needed (Holiness
Disguised), then shut their hearts, minds, and wallets;
No, each of these are but the lowliest

Of crimes. Tolerable, if I'm honest.
There's only one that pains my lungs to breathe,
Only one I deign to call a prophet.

There's only one that this could ever be:
I am the monster terrorizing me.

He Who Conquers, Conquered

June 20, 2026

Penitent patient, pillow a potent power,
Prayer to the the prayer's pay-per-the-hour power.

Purple protector, supple supplier of sleep,
Keep me breathing, releasing this awful power.

Crush me like velvet. Villify this villainess
Who, under duress, undressed and blessed my power.

I confess. I relapse to the Goddess of the Gaps.
Athena may rest, Arachne knew best: Power.

There's no sacred sigil, there's no secret potion,
There is only violence, dominance, power.

Granted by the minute like a genie, bottled
For eternity. Bounded eternal power.

The notion that Pandora's box could be opened
Yet misunderstood is an alluring power.

"I can fix her," one might say. "Mending broken locks
Is all I know. I'm the key. I have the power."

But I am no key. Jimmy the Meek. Pariah.
Pious Dutton. Lover. Rejector of Power.

Ritual ala Rondeau

June 18, 2026

Hegemonic or demonic, pray
The ritual, the pipe smoking gray
And lavenderly clouds of incense,
Calms your anxious nerves and your intense
But manageable pain. Those headaches

From crowded thoughts you can't shoo away
Brighten up the sun but darken day.
Crawling out of bed feels too immense.
Hegemonic or demonic, pray.

Breathe it deeply. Hold it, let it stay,
Then exhale and watch it dissipate.
Gentle wings alleviate and lift
From fog to cloud, offering a glimpse
Of peacefulness. But you won't escape
The ritual, the pipe smoking gray.

Ode to a Red Admiral

June 17, 2026

Blooming roses, coral petals
Sweet aroma, thorny brambles
Butterfly hovering, wandering, gathering
Pollen, pollinate and procreate,
Pro creator

Light upon leaf,
The dewy weight a sip to drink, you
Stretch your wings
    Silhouette gilded by morning sunlight
    Streaks of red along the length
    Spots of white like dandelion seeds
        Floating
Are they purple like the evening sky
    Or black like this ink?

I long to hear your story
To know the feeling of
    Wind raising me,
    Gently carrying me
    To fields of goldenrod
        To feast and flitter
        Among the golden glitter
    Where I can meet my love
    And live out my days
    Unfettered
So speak to me
And I shall listen.

Notre Dame

June 16, 2026

Should I attend Notre Dame?
This reverie brought upon
My subconscious -- Nostradam-
Us would envy you, grandma.

Notre Dame, the Catholic
University, admits
9% of graduates
From their pool of applicants.

Founded 1842
By congregants of Holy
Cross and named Notre Dame du
Lac; the Potawatomi

Chief Leopold Pokagon
Invited Stephen Badin
To preach, and he bought the land
And offered it to Sorin,

Edward, on the condition
He built on it a college
In two years. Eight from Ireland
And France offered their knowledge.

The General Assembly
Of Indiana chartered
It, making state history.
Ivy League, but no Harvard.

The Cats Meow

June 13, 2026

Rouse me from restless slumber
These semi-permanent fixtures
Fixated on midnight toe spasms
    And finger twitches
Pouncing,
Practice to catch the
    Mouse
    Bunny
    Bird
Greeting them through
Impenetrable glass.

Whispers from beyond
The sealed threshold
From semi-permanent fixtures
Semi-transparent like ghosts
    Or glass
    Or water
    All liquid in various states of flow
Now flowing freely and running
    Dry.

These responsibilities reminding me
Impermanence is a kind of permanence
Like a depression
    In a pillow or carpet —
Another flowing liquid, memory,
Reminding me to fill their cup
Lest they go thirsty, and

Rouse me from restless slumber.

In Darkness

June 12, 2026

In dark room I write
Fountain nib scratching white space black
Between blue lines

A rules follower, I ride
Placid currents, like surfing on the surface
Of a swimming pool, solo

What will you tell them about me
    Many years from now
    When I have seen them go out sailing
        From stranger shores
        On stronger seas
        With stringers strung and swaying
            And hungry for fresh fish
    While I have stayed stationery
    And solo?

Writing in a dark room,
Time ticking. Song ends.
Nothing new to say.

You say nothing new because nothing new exists
In darkness.

Truth Is

June 10, 2026

What do I know about Truth?
I believe it is simple
It represents the experiences we share
Like the sweetness of a watermelon
Snipped from the vine
Sliced into cubes or wedges or half-moons
    That fit perfect in our grasping hands
    So we can run and eat
    Wearing the rind like a toothy grin

Or it is the communal fear and thrill of firsts

Like driving
Gripping the steering wheel, the
Humming engine vibrates through our fingertips, and
Releasing the brake pedal
After selecting a gear
Sends vibrations to the wheels,
Which turn in proportion
To the pressure
Imposed
Upon the accelerator

Thing is
I do not believe Truth is simple

Truth is simple

Truth is simply these feelings we all carry
But our feelings
Inform our Truths

Truth is
There is no Universal Truth

Truth is a spectrum of colors
Viewed under different light
Electric or gas

Exchange

June 8, 2026

Morning blue gray
Weeping
Untreated wooden slat fence
Swells, bows, cracks, splinters

Property ends, property begins
Fence remains, fence rests, fence retires

Door opens, door closes
Woman emerges, dog emerges
Dog wanders.

Snapshots
Dog one frame, then panel
Like video buffering

Door opens, door closes
Woman retreats, dog retreats

Clucking
Morning song, mourning song?
New life or food, the egg?
Splashing, squelching
Flooded mud impacted, compacted
Footprint fans, footprints ferns fossilized

Weeping subsides
Morning bright blue, bright yellow, bright green

Crickets clicking, chickens chattering
Retreat inside, exchange heat for home
And home for life
